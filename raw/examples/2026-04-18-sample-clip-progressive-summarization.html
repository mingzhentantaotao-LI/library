Progressive Summarization for Personal Knowledge

Saved from a learning blog for later review.

The practical idea is to keep original material intact, then add lighter layers of summary on top of it instead of rewriting the source.

Layer 1 captures the full source. Layer 2 extracts a brief summary. Layer 3 promotes the most reusable ideas into evergreen notes.

The method works best when each note links forward to the synthesis and backward to the original evidence.

When time is limited, summarize only materials that are likely to be reused in future projects.
